Are these real people?

Yes — every member on Pro Love Hub is a real, verified person. We don't use stock photos or fake profiles. Each profile is reviewed by our team before it appears on the site.

Yes. Our members live across 40+ cities in Africa, Europe, North America, and the Middle East. You can choose to meet someone in your own city or anywhere in the world.

How do introductions work?

When you request an introduction, we personally reach out to the member you've chosen. Once they confirm they're interested, we connect you both on WhatsApp. No cold messages, no awkward first contact.

Browse our members, find someone who interests you, and open their profile. From there you'll see how to request an introduction. It takes less than a minute.
